# YayaAiki Investor Presentation Script

**Format:** 10-slide launch presentation script  
**Target duration:** 8–10 minutes  
**Audience:** early-stage investors, strategic partners, and Nigerian enterprise customers  
**Positioning:** Nigeria-first Work Assurance Infrastructure  
**Evidence rule:** Replace bracketed targets with measured pilot results before presenting them as facts.

## Slide 1 — Accepted work, not just access to workers

**On-slide message:**

> **YayaAiki turns business demand into accepted, auditable work.**

**Speaker script:**

Businesses do not ultimately buy access to workers. They buy work that is completed, accepted, and paid for with confidence.

YayaAiki is building a Nigeria-first Work Assurance platform that turns a business requirement into a structured Work Order, organizes qualified capacity, captures delivery evidence, verifies the result, records buyer acceptance, confirms payment through a licensed provider, and produces a Work Assurance Record.

Our initial product is not a generic freelancer marketplace. It is the operating layer between business demand and trusted economic outcomes.

## Slide 2 — The problem is not finding a person

**On-slide message:**

> **The work process breaks between request and outcome.**

**Speaker script:**

A business may find someone who can perform a task, but that does not answer the questions that matter afterward.

What exactly was requested? What output was expected? What made it acceptable? What evidence was delivered? Who checked it? What changed during rework? Was the buyer’s acceptance recorded? Was payment actually completed? Can the same workflow be repeated next month?

Today those facts are often scattered across messages, spreadsheets, files, and informal trust. The result is rework, delayed delivery, payment disputes, and no durable record of what happened.

## Slide 3 — Why Nigeria first

**On-slide message:**

> **A large informal work economy needs better proof of completed work.**

**Speaker script:**

Nigeria is the right starting market because it combines a large, informal, and self-employed labour force with growing digital work and payment activity. Businesses already outsource research, data processing, customer operations, catalogue work, and other repeatable tasks.

The opportunity is real, but it is not automatically a business. We are treating the market evidence as a demand hypothesis that must be proven through paid work, acceptance, contribution margin, and repeat orders.

Nigeria also gives us a focused operating environment: one initial jurisdiction, one currency, one support model, and a clear set of buyer and professional workflows before country expansion.

## Slide 4 — The product loop

**On-slide message:**

`Brief → Work Order → Capacity → Delivery → Evidence → Verification → Acceptance → Payment → Assurance Record`

**Speaker script:**

A buyer arrives through the website, WhatsApp, or a referral and starts a Work Order. They describe the outcome, quantity, deadline, acceptance requirements, evidence needed, data sensitivity, and budget range.

YayaAiki converts that request into a structured work order. Behind the self-service interface, our operations team can initially qualify the task, select capacity, supervise delivery, review evidence, and manage exceptions manually.

The customer still experiences one coherent product: a clear brief, transparent status, evidence, acceptance, payment state, and a downloadable record.

## Slide 5 — The first launch wedge

**On-slide message:**

> **Start with repeatable work where acceptance can be measured.**

**Speaker script:**

We are not starting with every type of work or every African country. We are testing a small number of repeatable task families, including data operations, AI evaluation, transcription, translation, research support, catalogue operations, and quality assurance.

The first buyers are organizations already purchasing work in batches: AI and data companies, research agencies, e-commerce operators, BPOs, NGOs, and enterprise operations teams.

Each pilot has one workflow, one defined volume, one acceptance standard, one price, one delivery window, and one Work Assurance Record.

## Slide 6 — Why existing marketplaces do not solve the complete problem

**On-slide message:**

> **Marketplaces answer “who can do this?” YayaAiki answers “can this work be trusted from request to outcome?”**

**Speaker script:**

Upwork, Workana, Andela, Jobberman, TERAWORK, Gebeya, and specialist compliance providers already address important parts of talent access, recruitment, screening, payments, or verification.

We do not claim those capabilities are absent from the market. We are building a different combination around the work record itself: structured specification, criteria-level evidence, independent verification, buyer acceptance, payment reconciliation, and a portable but permissioned record.

Our advantage will not come from saying we are first. It will come from proving that buyers use the record to reduce risk, accept work faster, repeat the workflow, and make better capacity decisions.

## Slide 7 — The moat hypothesis

**On-slide message:**

> **Every accepted work order creates governed evidence about quality, cost, time, and outcome.**

**Speaker script:**

The software is copyable. The long-term moat hypothesis is the Work Evidence Graph created through real economic activity.

Every accepted order adds structured evidence linking a specification to a capability, deliverable, reviewer decision, rework pattern, payment result, and buyer outcome. Over time, this can improve task templates, routing, reviewer calibration, pricing, quality prediction, and buyer trust.

The moat does not exist on day one. It must be earned through thousands of buyer-confirmed outcomes, trusted governance, workflow integrations, and a network of buyers, professionals, reviewers, and payment partners.

## Slide 8 — Business model and operating discipline

**On-slide message:**

> **Buyer-funded pilots first. Positive contribution margin before expansion.**

**Speaker script:**

Our initial model is buyer-funded. Revenue can combine a work-order service margin, verification or assurance fees, and later business subscriptions or enterprise integrations.

We will not optimize for registrations. For every work order we measure buyer revenue, professional payout, reviewer minutes, operations and support time, payment cost, rework, disputes, delivery time, acceptance, and contribution margin.

The first economic test is simple: can a repeatable workflow produce positive contribution margin after professional payout, review, support, payment, fraud, dispute, and acquisition costs?

We will not hold customer funds in Phase 1. Payment execution remains with licensed providers.

## Slide 9 — 30-day traction plan

**On-slide message:**

`100 accounts → 10+ real requests → 3–5 paid pilots → 2+ repeat customers`

**Speaker script:**

The first 30 days are designed to generate evidence, not vanity growth.

We will target 100 Nigerian organizations across five priority sectors, use WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, email, LinkedIn selectively, and referrals, and make **Start a Work Order** the primary call to action.

Our internal targets are 10 or more real work requests, at least five qualified opportunities, three to five paid pilots, completed accepted work, and at least two repeat customers or written repeat commitments.

If a workflow does not produce repeat demand or positive economics, we will stop or refine it rather than force it into the roadmap.

## Slide 10 — The ask and the proof we will earn

**On-slide message:**

> **Help us turn the first accepted work into a trusted operating layer.**

**Speaker script:**

The immediate objective is to prove one reliable economic loop in Nigeria: a real business brings real work, a professional delivers, evidence is reviewed, the buyer accepts, payment is confirmed, and the buyer orders again.

We are seeking early partners, anchor customers, and appropriately timed capital to strengthen the persistent Work Order loop, secure evidence handling, reviewer operations, payment reconciliation, and buyer acquisition.

Before claiming a category win, we will earn the proof: paid customers, completed work, acceptance accuracy, rework and dispute rates, payout reliability, positive contribution margin, repeat orders, and a governed evidence graph.

YayaAiki’s long-term vision is continental, but the launch discipline is local:

> **Nigeria first. Buyer-funded. Self-service at the front door. Human-assisted behind the interface. Evidence before claims. Repeat Assured Work before expansion.**

## Closing sentence

> **YayaAiki is building the layer that makes economic work measurable, provable, verifiable, and transferable from request to accepted outcome.**

## Investor diligence guardrails

Do not present the following as existing facts until verified:

- customer count;
- completed work orders;
- revenue or gross services volume;
- worker earnings;
- acceptance or verification accuracy;
- take rate or gross margin;
- payment-provider licence or country coverage;
- customer retention;
- category leadership;
- first-mover status;
- pan-African availability;
- regulatory compliance certification.

Label them as **targets**, **hypotheses**, or **pilot results** until evidence exists.
