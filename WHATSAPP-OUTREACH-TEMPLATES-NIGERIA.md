# YayaAiki WhatsApp Outreach Templates: Nigeria

**Purpose:** Start conversations about recurring work and move qualified prospects to **Start a Work Order**.  
**Rule:** Personalize every message. Do not bulk-send, scrape contact lists, or imply that YayaAiki already has verified capacity, customers, payment partners, or results that have not been proven.

## Universal short opener

> Hi [Name], I came across [Company] and noticed you work in [specific area]. I’m building YayaAiki to help Nigerian businesses get repeatable operational work done with a clear brief, evidence, verification, acceptance, and a work record — not just a freelancer introduction. If you regularly outsource [specific task], you can send one real requirement here or start a Work Order at [yayaaiki.com/start-work]. Would that be relevant to [Company]?

## 1. AI, data, and machine-learning companies

### First message

> Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] works with [AI/data product, datasets, model evaluation, or data operations]. Teams doing this work often lose time on unclear task instructions, inconsistent labels, QA rework, and weak delivery evidence. YayaAiki is testing a Nigeria-first Work Assurance workflow: you define the task and acceptance criteria, we organize capacity, collect evidence, verify the output, and return a clear work record. If you have a recurring batch such as classification, response evaluation, transcription, or QA, you can send the brief directly at [yayaaiki.com/start-work]. Would you be open to testing one paid batch?

### Follow-up

> Hi [Name], following up with a specific question: what repeatable data or QA task does [Company] currently spend the most time supervising? We are looking for one controlled paid pilot where the output and acceptance standard can be measured clearly. You can send the requirement without booking a demo: [yayaaiki.com/start-work].

### Qualification prompt

> To scope it accurately, what is the approximate batch size, expected accuracy, delivery window, and evidence you need from the team doing the work?

## Response handling

### If the prospect is interested

> Great. Please send the task, expected output, approximate volume, deadline, acceptance requirements, evidence needed, data sensitivity, and budget range here: [yayaaiki.com/start-work]. We’ll return an intake ID and review the requirement before confirming scope, capacity, price, or timeline.

### If the prospect asks for a demo

> Happy to show it. The fastest way to make the conversation useful is to start with one real recurring task. You can submit it here first: [yayaaiki.com/start-work]. If anything is unclear, I’ll help refine the Work Order from there.

### If the prospect asks whether capacity is already available

> We are building the professional network around qualified demand, so I do not want to promise capacity before reviewing the task. Send the requirement and we’ll confirm the appropriate delivery plan after qualification.

### If the prospect asks for a free trial

> We are running controlled paid pilots so we can measure quality, delivery time, review effort, and repeat value properly. We can keep the first batch small and clearly scoped, with a defined acceptance standard.

### If the prospect is not the right contact

> Understood. Who owns [operations/data/research/procurement] and regularly buys or coordinates this type of work? A referral would be appreciated, and I can send them a short description rather than a general company pitch.

### Opt-out

> Understood — I won’t follow up again. Thank you for letting me know.
